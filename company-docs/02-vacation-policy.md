# BergTech Maschinenbau GmbH — Vacation and Absence Policy

**Document Type:** Internal HR Policy
**Version:** 3.4
**Last Updated:** 15.01.2026
**Owner:** HR Department, BergTech Maschinenbau GmbH

---

## 1. Annual Vacation Entitlement

Full-time employees at BergTech are entitled to **30 working days of paid vacation per calendar year**. Part-time employees receive a prorated entitlement based on their contracted weekly hours.

### Additional Days
- After 10 years of service: +1 additional day
- After 20 years of service: +2 additional days
- Employees with severe disability (Schwerbehinderung): +5 additional days

### Probationary Period
New employees become eligible for vacation **after completing the 6-month probationary period**. Vacation accrued during this period can be used afterwards.

---

## 2. Vacation Request Procedure

### Advance Notice Requirements

| Vacation Length | Minimum Advance Notice |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| 1-2 days | At least **5 working days** in advance |
| 3-10 days | At least **3 weeks** in advance |
| More than 10 days | At least **6 weeks** in advance |

### Request Process

1. Submit your vacation request via the **BergTech HR Portal** under "Time Off > New Request".
2. Your direct manager receives an automatic notification and reviews the request.
3. Approval or rejection is communicated within **5 working days**.
4. Once approved, the absence is automatically synced with your work calendar.

### Approval Considerations

Vacation requests are approved based on:
- Operational needs of the department
- Coverage availability among team members
- Project deadlines and customer commitments
- Order of submission ("first come, first served")

---

## 3. Mandatory Vacation Periods

BergTech requires all employees to take **at least 12 consecutive working days** of vacation per year (per § 7 Bundesurlaubsgesetz). This typically falls in the summer or year-end period.

### Company Closure
BergTech is closed during these mandatory periods:
- **Christmas / New Year:** December 24th to January 1st (5 working days, deducted from vacation entitlement)
- **Summer Plant Maintenance:** First two weeks of August (varies yearly, deducted from vacation entitlement)

---

## 4. Vacation Carryover

- Unused vacation days from the current year may be carried over to the next year **only until March 31st**.
- Vacation days not taken by March 31st of the following year **expire** unless specific exceptions apply (e.g., long-term illness).
- A maximum of **10 days** can be carried over.

---

## 5. Sick Leave (Krankmeldung)

### Reporting Procedure

1. Notify your direct manager **before the start of the workday** by phone or email.
2. From **day 1 of illness**, an employee notification (Krankmeldung) is mandatory.
3. From **day 4 of illness**, a medical certificate (Arbeitsunfähigkeitsbescheinigung) must be submitted to HR.

### Submission of Medical Certificate
- Via the HR Portal (digital upload)
- By email to krankmeldung@bergtech.de
- In person at the HR office

### Continued Pay
Employees receive full salary for up to **6 weeks** of illness per illness instance. Beyond that, statutory sick pay (Krankengeld) from the health insurance applies.

---

## 6. Special Leave (Sonderurlaub)

Paid special leave is granted for the following events:

| Event | Days |
|-------|------|
| Own wedding | 2 days |
| Birth of a child (partner) | 2 days |
| Death of immediate family member | 3 days |
| Move (own household, not job-related) | 1 day |
| Doctor's appointments (if not possible outside work) | as needed |
| Court summons or official appointments | as needed |

Special leave must be requested in advance via the HR Portal with appropriate documentation.

---

## 7. Parental Leave (Elternzeit)

- Parental leave can be requested for up to 3 years per child.
- Notification must be submitted to HR **at least 7 weeks before the planned start**.
- During parental leave, the employment contract is suspended; statutory parental allowance (Elterngeld) applies.

---

## 8. Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave (Unbezahlter Urlaub) of up to **3 months** can be granted in exceptional cases (e.g., sabbatical, family care). Requests must be submitted **3 months in advance** and require approval by both the direct manager and HR.

---

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I take vacation right after my probation ends?**
A: Yes, once the 6-month probation is completed, full vacation entitlement applies retroactively.

**Q: What happens if my vacation request is rejected?**
A: Your manager will provide reasons. You may submit a revised request or escalate to HR if needed.

**Q: Can I cancel an approved vacation?**
A: Yes, but only via the HR Portal with at least 5 working days' notice. Late cancellations require written justification.

**Q: Do public holidays count as vacation days?**
A: No. Public holidays falling on a workday are non-working days and do not deduct from your vacation entitlement.
