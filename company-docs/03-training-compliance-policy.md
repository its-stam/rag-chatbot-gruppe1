# BergTech Maschinenbau GmbH — Training and Compliance Policy

**Document Type:** Internal HR Policy
**Version:** 4.0
**Last Updated:** 10.03.2026

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy defines the mandatory and recommended training programs at BergTech Maschinenbau GmbH. It applies to all employees, including temporary staff, contractors with long-term assignments (>6 months), and apprentices.

The policy ensures compliance with:
- German Occupational Health and Safety Act (Arbeitsschutzgesetz)
- ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO)
- IT Security Standards (BSI Grundschutz)
- Anti-Corruption Laws

---

## 2. Mandatory Trainings for New Employees (Onboarding)

All new employees must complete the following five trainings within their **first 30 days** of employment:

### 2.1 Workplace Safety Training
- **Duration:** 4 hours (online)
- **Content:** Emergency procedures, fire safety, first aid basics, ergonomics, safe handling of equipment
- **Renewal:** Annually
- **Provider:** BergTech LMS (Learning Management System)

### 2.2 Data Protection (GDPR/DSGVO) Compliance
- **Duration:** 2 hours (online)
- **Content:** Personal data handling, data subject rights, breach reporting, employee responsibilities
- **Renewal:** Every 2 years
- **Provider:** BergTech LMS

### 2.3 IT Security Awareness
- **Duration:** 2 hours (online)
- **Content:** Phishing detection, password management, secure remote work, mobile device security
- **Renewal:** Annually
- **Provider:** BergTech LMS

### 2.4 Anti-Corruption and Code of Conduct
- **Duration:** 1 hour (online)
- **Content:** Bribery prevention, conflict of interest, gift policy, whistleblower process
- **Renewal:** Every 2 years
- **Provider:** BergTech LMS

### 2.5 Quality Management System (ISO 9001) Introduction
- **Duration:** 3 hours (in-person)
- **Content:** Quality principles at BergTech, document control, non-conformance reporting
- **Renewal:** Every 3 years
- **Provider:** Quality Management Department

**Tracking:** Completion is automatically logged in the LMS and reported monthly to the HR Compliance Officer.

## 3. Role-Specific Mandatory Trainings

Depending on your role, additional trainings are mandatory:

| Role | Additional Trainings | Frequency |
|------|---------------------|-----------|
| Production / Workshop | Machine Operation Certification, PPE Training, Hazardous Materials | Annually |
| Engineering / R&D | Intellectual Property Protection, CAD Standards, Patent Awareness | Every 2 years |
| Sales / Procurement | Anti-Trust Law, Export Control, Contract Negotiation Ethics | Annually |
| Management | Leadership Essentials, Performance Management, Labor Law Basics | Every 2 years |
| IT / Software | Secure Coding, Penetration Testing Awareness, ISO 27001 Basics | Annually |
| HR | Discrimination Prevention, Recruitment Law, Confidentiality | Annually |

---

## 4. Annual Mandatory Refresher Trainings

All employees must complete the following annually, regardless of role:

1. Workplace Safety Refresher (1 hour)
2. IT Security Refresher (1 hour)
3. Compliance Updates (1 hour)
4. Fire Safety Drill Participation (in-person, 30 min)

**Deadline:** All annual refreshers must be completed by **December 31st** of each calendar year.

---

## 5. Voluntary and Career Development Trainings

BergTech encourages continuous professional development. Employees may request additional trainings through the HR Portal under "Training > Development Request".

### Funding
- **Up to €1,500 per year** is available for individual training budgets.
- Requests above €1,500 require approval by the direct manager and HR.
- Internal trainings are free of charge.

### Examples of Voluntary Trainings
- Foreign language courses (English, French, Mandarin)
- Project Management certification (PMP, Prince2, Scrum)
- Technical certifications (AWS, Azure, SAP)
- Soft skills (Communication, Conflict Management)

## 6. Compliance Reporting and Tracking

### Tracking System
All training records are stored in the BergTech LMS and accessible to:
- The employee (own records)
- The direct manager (team records)
- HR Compliance (full overview)

### Monthly Compliance Reports
HR Compliance issues monthly reports to the Executive Board, identifying:
- Overdue trainings
- Departments with low compliance
- Training effectiveness metrics

### Consequences of Non-Compliance
- **First missed deadline:** Reminder via email
- **Second missed deadline:** Direct manager notified
- **Third missed deadline:** Formal warning issued; access to certain systems may be revoked

## 8. Training Records and Audits

- Employees can download their training certificates from the LMS at any time.
- BergTech is audited annually by external ISO 9001 and internal compliance auditors.
- Training records are retained for **10 years** in compliance with statutory requirements.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What happens if I miss a mandatory training?**
A: You will receive a reminder. Three missed deadlines result in a formal warning.

**Q: Can I skip onboarding trainings if I have prior certifications?**
A: No. BergTech-specific trainings are mandatory regardless of prior experience. External certifications can be considered for voluntary modules.

**Q: Who do I contact if my training records are incorrect?**
A: Contact HR Compliance at compliance@bergtech.de.

**Q: Are trainings counted as working time?**
A: Yes, all mandatory trainings are conducted during working hours.
