# BergTech Maschinenbau GmbH — Employee Offboarding Checklist

**Document Type:** Internal HR Policy
**Version:** 2.3
**Last Updated:** 05.02.2026
**Owner:** HR Department, BergTech Maschinenbau GmbH

---

## 1. Purpose

This document outlines the standard offboarding process for employees leaving BergTech Maschinenbau GmbH, whether through resignation, retirement, contract end, or termination. The goal is to ensure a smooth, respectful, and legally compliant exit for both the employee and the company.

---

## 2. Notice Periods

### Standard Notice Periods (§ 622 BGB)

| Length of Service | Notice Period |
|-------------------|---------------|
| During probationary period | 2 weeks |
| Less than 2 years | 4 weeks to the 15th or end of month |
| 2-5 years | 1 month to end of month |
| 5-8 years | 2 months to end of month |
| 8-10 years | 3 months to end of month |
| 10-12 years | 4 months to end of month |
| 12-15 years | 5 months to end of month |
| 15-20 years | 6 months to end of month |
| 20+ years | 7 months to end of month |

### Resignation Procedure
1. Submit a **signed written resignation letter** to HR (email or in person).
2. HR confirms receipt and notice period within **3 working days**.
3. The resignation date is entered into the HR system, triggering the offboarding workflow.

## 3. Offboarding Workflow Timeline

### 4 Weeks Before Last Working Day

- [ ] HR sends the official Offboarding Confirmation Letter
- [ ] HR schedules the Exit Interview (last 2 weeks of employment)
- [ ] Direct manager initiates Knowledge Transfer Plan
- [ ] Replacement hiring or task redistribution begins
- [ ] Open vacation balance reviewed and scheduled

### 2 Weeks Before Last Working Day

- [ ] Knowledge Transfer Sessions with successor or team
- [ ] Documentation of ongoing projects, tasks, and key contacts
- [ ] Handover of customer relationships and external accounts
- [ ] Update of project documentation in BergTech systems

### 1 Week Before Last Working Day

- [ ] Exit Interview with HR (typically 60 minutes)
- [ ] Final Performance Conversation with direct manager
- [ ] Reference Letter draft prepared by manager (qualifiziertes Arbeitszeugnis)
- [ ] IT begins access deactivation planning

### Last Working Day

- [ ] Return all BergTech property (see Section 4)
- [ ] All access rights revoked at end of day (badges, accounts, VPN)
- [ ] Final farewell with team
- [ ] Receipt of Reference Letter
- [ ] Receipt of Final Documents (see Section 5)

### After Last Working Day

- [ ] Final salary payment (28th of the month following last day)
- [ ] Vacation payout if not fully used
- [ ] Pension scheme transfer documentation
- [ ] Tax certificate (Lohnsteuerbescheinigung) sent by end of February of following year

---

## 4. Items to Return on Last Working Day

The departing employee is responsible for returning the following items to the appropriate departments:

### To IT Department
- Laptop and all peripherals (charger, dock, mouse, keyboard, headset)
- Mobile phone and SIM card (if provided)
- External hard drives and USB devices
- Software licenses (transferred or revoked)

### To HR / Facilities
- Access card (badge)
- Keys (office, drawers, lockers)
- Parking card
- Cafeteria card

### To Department / Team
- Business documents and files
- BergTech-branded equipment (laptop bags, etc.)
- Books, tools, and resources from team library

### To Production / Workshop (if applicable)
- Work clothing and safety shoes
- Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Tools allocated to the role
- Workshop key card

A signed **Return of Property Form** is completed by HR and the employee.

---

## 5. Final Documents Provided to Employee

On the last working day, the employee receives:

1. **Reference Letter (Arbeitszeugnis)** — qualified letter detailing role, performance, and conduct
2. **Final Salary Statement (Gehaltsabrechnung)** — at the end of the final pay period
3. **Confirmation of Employment (Arbeitsbescheinigung)** — for the Federal Employment Agency
4. **Pension Scheme Documentation (bAV)** — for transfer or continuation
5. **Health Insurance Notification (Abmeldebescheinigung)**
6. **Tax Certificate (Lohnsteuerbescheinigung)** — sent by end of February of following year

---

## 6. Knowledge Transfer Requirements

To ensure business continuity, the following knowledge must be transferred before the last working day:

- [ ] Active project status and next steps
- [ ] Key customer and supplier contacts
- [ ] Internal process documentation
- [ ] Login credentials for shared systems (passed to manager, not successor)
- [ ] Pending decisions or open items
- [ ] Risk areas and known issues
- [ ] Team relationships and communication norms

A **Knowledge Transfer Document** must be uploaded to the team's shared folder before the last working day.

## 7. Exit Interview

The Exit Interview is conducted by a member of the HR Department in the final 2 weeks of employment.

### Purpose
- Gather feedback on the work experience
- Identify reasons for leaving
- Improve retention and culture
- Address any open concerns

### Format
- Duration: approximately 60 minutes
- Confidential (results aggregated, not shared with manager directly)
- Voluntary participation, but strongly encouraged

### Topics Covered
- Reason for leaving
- Job satisfaction and team dynamics
- Onboarding experience reflection
- Feedback on management and culture
- Suggestions for improvement
- Future career plans (optional)

## 8. Special Cases

### Termination by Employer
- Notice periods follow the same § 622 BGB structure.
- A separation agreement (Aufhebungsvertrag) may be offered.
- Garden leave (Freistellung) may be granted at BergTech's discretion.
- Works Council (Betriebsrat) is informed as required by law.

### Retirement (Renteneintritt)
- Notification at least 12 months in advance is requested.
- Retirement gift and farewell event organized by the team.
- Pension scheme transition support provided.

### Contract End (Befristung)
- Automatic termination on the contract end date.
- Possibility of extension or permanent contract assessed 3 months before end.
- Same offboarding checklist applies.

### Mutual Termination Agreement (Aufhebungsvertrag)
- Negotiated between employee and HR.
- Often includes severance payment (Abfindung).
- Legal counsel for the employee is recommended.

---

## 9. Confidentiality and Post-Employment Obligations

After leaving BergTech, the former employee must:
- Maintain confidentiality of trade secrets and internal information indefinitely
- Comply with non-compete clauses if applicable (max 2 years per § 74 HGB)
- Not solicit BergTech customers or employees for 12 months (where contractually agreed)

Violations may result in legal consequences including damages and contractual penalties.
