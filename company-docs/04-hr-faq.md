## Section A: Onboarding & First Steps

**Q1: What documents do I need to submit before my first working day?**
A: You must submit the following at least 5 working days before your start: signed employment contract, Tax ID, Social Security Number, health insurance confirmation, bank details, ID copy, educational certificates, and (if applicable) work permit/visa and driver's license.

**Q2: Where do I report on my first day?**
A: At the HR Office, Building A, Room 1.05, at 08:00. Frau Schneider from the HR Onboarding team will welcome you.

**Q3: Will I get a buddy?**
A: Yes, a designated Onboarding Buddy will accompany you during your first week. Your buddy is assigned by your direct manager.

**Q4: What equipment do I receive on my first day?**
A: A laptop, office or softphone, access card, BergTech email account, VPN access, and a Welcome Kit. Production roles also receive work clothes and PPE.

## Section B: Vacation & Time Off

**Q5: How many days in advance should vacation be requested?**
A: For 1-2 days: at least 5 working days in advance. For 3-10 days: 3 weeks in advance. For more than 10 days: 6 weeks in advance.

**Q6: How much annual vacation do I get?**
A: Full-time employees receive 30 working days per year. Long-term service and severe disability add additional days.

**Q7: Can I carry over unused vacation to the next year?**
A: Yes, up to 10 days can be carried over until March 31st of the following year. After that, unused days expire.

**Q8: When am I eligible for vacation as a new employee?**
A: After completing the 6-month probationary period. Vacation accrued during probation can be used afterwards.

**Q9: How do I report sick leave?**
A: Notify your manager by phone or email before the start of the workday. From day 4, submit a medical certificate to HR via the HR Portal or krankmeldung@bergtech.de.

**Q10: How long do I receive full pay during illness?**
A: Up to 6 weeks per illness instance. Beyond that, statutory sick pay (Krankengeld) applies.

## Section C: Trainings & Compliance

**Q11: Which mandatory trainings must new employees complete during onboarding?**
A: Five mandatory trainings within the first 30 days: Workplace Safety (4h), GDPR Compliance (2h), IT Security Awareness (2h), Anti-Corruption (1h), and ISO 9001 Introduction (3h).

**Q12: How often do trainings need to be renewed?**
A: Annually for safety, IT security, and compliance refreshers. Every 2 years for GDPR and Code of Conduct. Every 3 years for ISO 9001.

**Q13: Are trainings counted as working time?**
A: Yes, all mandatory trainings happen during paid working hours.

**Q14: What is my annual training budget?**
A: Up to €1,500 per year for voluntary professional development. Requests above this require manager and HR approval.

**Q15: What happens if I miss a mandatory training deadline?**
A: First miss: reminder. Second miss: manager notified. Third miss: formal warning and possible access revocation.

## Section D: Compensation & Benefits

**Q16: When is my salary paid?**
A: Salaries are paid on the 28th of each month for the current month. The first salary is prorated if you start mid-month.

**Q17: What benefits does BergTech offer?**
A: Subsidized cafeteria meals, JobTicket (public transport allowance €40/month), company pension scheme (bAV), bike leasing program (JobRad), and corporate fitness benefits.

**Q18: Is there a Christmas or vacation bonus?**
A: Yes. BergTech pays a 13th-month salary as Christmas bonus (paid in November) and a vacation bonus of 50% of monthly salary (paid in June).

**Q19: How do I get reimbursed for travel expenses?**
A: Submit a travel expense report via the HR Portal under "Travel > Expense Report" within 14 days of return. Required: receipts, itinerary, and approval by direct manager.

## Section E: Workplace & Operations

**Q20: Can I work from home?**
A: After your first 4 weeks of onboarding, remote work options can be discussed with your manager. The standard is hybrid: up to 2 days per week from home.

**Q21: What are my working hours?**
A: Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 7:30-16:30 with a 45-minute lunch break. Flexible working time (Gleitzeit) is available with core hours from 09:30-15:00.

**Q22: How do I record my working hours?**
A: Via the digital time tracking system in the BergTech HR Portal or via the access card terminal at building entrances.

**Q23: What is the dress code?**
A: Business casual for office roles. Production roles have specified work clothing including safety shoes and PPE.

## Section F: Career & Development

**Q24: How are promotions handled?**
A: Promotions are reviewed annually as part of the performance review cycle. Eligibility is based on performance, role availability, and skills development.

**Q25: Can I switch departments internally?**
A: Yes, internal transfers are possible after 12 months in your current role. Open positions are listed on the Internal Job Board.

**Q26: How is performance reviewed?**
A: Annual performance review with your direct manager (target setting + evaluation). Mid-year check-ins are recommended.

## Section G: Offboarding & Resignation

**Q27: What is the notice period?**
A: 4 weeks to the 15th or end of the month during the first 2 years; longer thereafter as per § 622 BGB. Probationary period: 2 weeks.

**Q28: How do I resign?**
A: Submit a written resignation letter (signed) to HR. Keep a copy for your records. Confirmation is provided within 3 working days.

**Q29: What happens to my unused vacation when I leave?**
A: Unused vacation must be taken before your last working day. If not possible, it is paid out with your final salary.

**Q30: Will I receive a reference letter?**
A: Yes, a qualified reference letter (qualifiziertes Arbeitszeugnis) is provided automatically upon termination.
