## 1. Welcome to BergTech

BergTech Maschinenbau GmbH is a medium-sized engineering company with 450 employees, founded in 1987 and headquartered in Stuttgart, Germany. We specialize in precision machinery for the automotive and aerospace industries. This guide will walk you through your first weeks at BergTech and help you settle in smoothly.

---

## 2. Documents to Submit Before Your First Working Day

Every new employee is required to submit the following documents to the HR department **at least 5 working days before their first working day**:

1. **Signed Employment Contract** (one copy returned to HR)
2. **Tax ID (Steueridentifikationsnummer)**
3. **Social Security Number (Sozialversicherungsausweis)**
4. **Health Insurance Confirmation (Mitgliedsbescheinigung)**
5. **Bank Account Details (IBAN/BIC)**
6. **Copy of ID Card or Passport**
7. **Highest Educational Certificate (Diploma, Apprenticeship Certificate, etc.)**
8. **Work Permit or Visa** (only for non-EU citizens)
9. **Driver's License** (only for positions requiring driving)

Documents can be submitted via the secure HR Portal or in person at the HR office (Building A, Room 1.05).

## 4. Probationary Period (Probezeit)

The probationary period at BergTech is **6 months**, during which both parties may terminate the contract with a notice period of 2 weeks. During the probationary period:

- A monthly feedback meeting with your direct manager is mandatory.
- After 3 months, a formal mid-probation review is conducted.
- After 5 months, a final probation review determines continued employment.

---

## 5. Mandatory Trainings During Onboarding

All new employees must complete the following trainings within the first 30 days:

1. **Workplace Safety Training** (4 hours, online)
2. **Data Protection (GDPR) Compliance** (2 hours, online)
3. **IT Security Awareness** (2 hours, online)
4. **Anti-Corruption and Code of Conduct** (1 hour, online)
5. **Quality Management System (ISO 9001) Introduction** (3 hours, in-person)

Training completion is tracked in the LMS and reported to HR Compliance.

## 8. Frequently Asked First-Week Questions

**Q: When will I receive my first salary?**
A: Salaries are paid on the 28th of each month for the current month. If you start mid-month, your first salary is prorated.

**Q: Can I start working remotely from Day 1?**
A: No. The first 4 weeks must be completed onsite to ensure proper onboarding. Remote work options are discussed with your manager after Week 4.

**Q: When am I eligible for vacation?**
A: After completing the probationary period of 6 months. Refer to the Vacation and Absence Policy for details.

**Q: Where do I find internal documents and forms?**
A: All HR forms are available on the BergTech Intranet under HR Portal > Forms.
